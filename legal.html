Privacy Policy

1. General Information

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have. This website is operated by Everbloom Gaming (Ruby Grube, Am Langen Felde 20, 30900 Wedemark, Germany).

2. Data Collection — Application Form

When you submit an application to join one of our rosters, we collect the following data on a voluntary basis:

  • Your first name (real name)
  • Your Discord username & In-Game Tag
  • Your current and peak Valorant rank
  • Your age and previous team experience

This data is used exclusively to evaluate your application and contact you regarding roster decisions. It will not be passed on to third parties.

3. Data Collection — Scrim Request Form

When you submit a scrim request via our website, we collect the following data:

  • Your organization or team name
  • Your average rank
  • Your Discord tag
  • Your preferred match time

This data is transmitted directly to our Discord server via a webhook and is used solely for the purpose of coordinating practice matches. It is not stored on our servers and not passed on to third parties.

4. External Services — Elfsight

Our website uses the Elfsight widget service to display our X (Twitter) feed and an AI chat assistant. These widgets are only loaded once you have given your consent via the cookie banner. Once loaded, Elfsight may collect technical data such as your IP address and browser information.

If you use the chat assistant, the messages you type are transmitted to Elfsight and processed by an AI service in order to generate a response. Please do not enter sensitive personal information into the chat. The assistant is automated — its answers may be inaccurate and do not constitute binding statements by Everbloom Gaming.

This is subject to Elfsight's Privacy Policy. We have no influence over the data collected by Elfsight.

5. Cookies and Consent

We use Cookiebot by Usercentrics to obtain and manage your consent for cookies and third-party services. When you first visit our site, a banner lets you accept or decline. Services that are not strictly necessary — such as the Elfsight widgets described above — remain blocked until you consent.

Cookiebot stores your choice so the banner does not reappear on every visit, and processes a hashed identifier together with technical data for this purpose. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time using the link in the banner. For details see Cookiebot's Privacy Policy.
